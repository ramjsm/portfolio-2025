<Intro>

_Sing or Sink_ is a real time interactive installation developed as an experiment with voice and interaction. Created during a three day working residency, the piece uses a 360° dome to create a landscape that moves from a dense, dark underworld at the bottom toward an open sky above. As visitors sing, their voices carry them upward through the environment. When the voices stop, they sink back down, making sustained singing necessary to reach the sky.

The installation was built in Unity, using NDI to stream real time interactive graphics onto the 360° dome projection. The project was developed collaboratively by the team during [Experimental Commons](https://www.instagram.com/p/DX6p_ukKs_Y/) working residency organized by [CCCBerlin](https://www.creative-cross-collaborations.de/home) and [A MAZE.](https://2026.amaze-berlin.de/)

</Intro>
