<Intro>

_Organismus_ is a projection mapping work that reflects on our relationship with nature and the systems we build around us. It approaches nature as a living system that we are intrinsically a part of.

The work brings together graphic elements, architectural patterns, organic forms, and natural landscapes. Mechanical structures gradually become intertwined with natural forms, connecting the constructed and the organic. The movement explores cycles of growth, transformation, and renewal, reflecting on the interconnection between different systems and forms of existence.

_Organismus_ was developed collaboratively by the team and presented as a projection mapping work at the [Sehsüchte Film Festival 2026](https://sehsuechte.de/en/programme/projection-mapping-friday/s), whose 55th edition was centered around the question _“What's left?”_. In this context, the work reflects on what remains when the structures we build begin to change, and on the possibility that life, connection, and consciousness might persist through their transformation.

</Intro>
