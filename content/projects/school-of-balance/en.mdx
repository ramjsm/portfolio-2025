<Intro>

_The School of Balance_ is a web based experiment exploring flocking behavior through sound and interaction. Inspired by [Reynolds' 1986 Boids algorithm](https://www.red3d.com/cwr/boids/), the project uses the three basic rules of separation, alignment, and cohesion to create a school of koi fish swimming together on screen, with [Wawa Sensei’s Boid tutorial](https://wawasensei.dev/tuto/boid-flocking-simulation-threejs-and-react) as a starting point.

The fish respond to audio captured through the visitor's microphone, scattering when noise disturbs the group. In collaboration with [Thibaut Bournazac](https://www.karde.me/thibautbournazac), we developed an evolving soundscape that explored states of tension and release, shaped by the movement of the flock and the audio input.

_The School of Balance_ was submitted as an entry to the [Aquarium Challenge](https://threejs-journey.com/challenges/019-aquarium#), part of the [Three.js Journey](https://threejs-journey.com/) student challenges. The project explores balance as a process of returning to harmony after disruption.

</Intro>
