<Intro>

_Juliette_ is a biomorphic interactive installation exploring the tension between desire, dependency, and physical proximity. Combining sculpture, projection mapping, and motion tracking, she responds in real time to the presence of visitors.

By presenting desire through an abstract, uncanny body, the installation plays with the boundary between body and object, attraction and discomfort.

Juliette's desire belongs to her, but she cannot fulfil it alone. This dependence creates a tension between autonomy and the need for another, making physical proximity part of the experience of pleasure.

The installation was created in collaboration with [Iria do Castelo](https://www.iriadocastelo.com/), combining her sculptural work with interactive and technological elements developed for the piece. Built in Unity, it uses projection mapping and real-time motion tracking, and was presented at [Un_Real Desires 2019](https://somos-arts.org/un_real-desires-group-exhibition/) at [SomoS Art House Berlin](https://somos-arts.org/) in 2019.

</Intro>
