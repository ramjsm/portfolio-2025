<Intro>

_The Post Human Shop_ is a speculative biotech shop presented through both a physical exhibition and an online platform. Promising to “transcend biological limitations”, it offers visitors the possibility of transforming the human body through a range of imagined genetic modifications.

The website provides the entry point into the fictional shop, where visitors browse AI-generated mutations, select a desired transformation, and book an appointment for a physical scan. The exhibition continues this process in space, materializing the shop's products through sculptures and extending its world through interactive works and performances.

The project explores the intersections of posthumanism, capitalism, and the human body through digital experimentation, interactive media, and material transformation.

_The Post Human Shop_ was conceptualized and developed by the team within the [54H Art Squat](https://www.instagram.com/54artsquat/) art creation rave and awarded a week long residency in 2026 to further develop the project. The work was presented during [Myths of Tomorrow](https://www.instagram.com/p/DO6R3EODFWS/) and [Vorspiel 2026](https://www.instagram.com/p/DTIhNZeDdHl/).

</Intro>
